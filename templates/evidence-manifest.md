## Confidence vocabulary

Apply the following labels to claims, with a rationale; they are not a substitute
for methodology or a guarantee of generality.

- `CONFIRMED`: directly verified under the stated conditions.
- `SUPPORTED`: evidence lends support but does not establish the full claim.
- `SELF-REPORTED`: based on a report not independently verified.
- `CONFLICTING`: material evidence disagrees; describe both sides.
- `UNVERIFIED`: insufficient evidence or verification not performed.
- `SUPERSEDED`: replaced by a specified later claim or record.
